# Governance

Handwriting Worksheet Generator is currently maintained under a lightweight maintainer-led model.

## Roles

- **Users** use the software and provide feedback.
- **Contributors** submit issues, documentation, designs, tests, or code.
- **Maintainers** review contributions, manage releases, enforce community standards, and make final project decisions.

Roles are based on sustained, constructive participation rather than employment or affiliation. As the community grows, maintainers may invite contributors with a strong record of helpful work to take on review or maintenance responsibilities.

## Decision Making

Routine decisions are made through issue and pull-request review. Significant changes should begin with a feature proposal and include:

- The problem being solved.
- User impact.
- Alternatives considered.
- Compatibility and migration concerns.
- Testing and documentation plans.

Maintainers seek practical consensus. When consensus is not possible, the maintainers make the final decision based on the project's goals, maintainability, accessibility, privacy, and print accuracy.

Architecture decisions with lasting consequences are recorded under `docs/architecture`.

## Releases

Maintainers are responsible for versioning, release notes, platform builds, signing, and publication. Automated checks must pass before a release is created.

## Changes to Governance

Governance changes are proposed and reviewed through pull requests.
