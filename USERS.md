# Who Is Using Handwriting Worksheet Generator?

This page is a voluntary showcase for people and organizations using the project. It helps contributors understand real-world needs and gives the project public, verifiable adoption examples.

No one is required to register or identify themselves in order to use the software.

## Add an Entry

Choose either:

1. Open a **Share your use** issue using the repository's issue chooser.
2. Open a pull request adding one short entry to the Community Users section.

You may identify yourself using a personal name, organization, GitHub username, or a general description such as “an elementary-school teacher in Ontario.” A website or logo is optional.

Only share information you are authorized to make public. Do not include student names, worksheet contents, private educational information, customer information, or proprietary fonts.

Entries may be edited for clarity and safety. Anyone listed may request that their entry be changed or removed.
